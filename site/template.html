About the data. For companies listed on NSE/BSE, the Indian‑vs‑foreign split is computed from the latest quarterly shareholding pattern the company filed with the exchange: foreign promoters, FII/FPI and other foreign institutions count as foreign; Indian promoters, DII, government and resident public count as Indian (NRIs are counted on the Indian side). Private companies use curated public records. The registry is rebuilt weekly; ownership can change between filings. This is general information, not investment advice.
